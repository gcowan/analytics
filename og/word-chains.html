For the grown-up

This is the single best five-minute drill for a child who reads a word by its first letter and guesses the rest. Say the target word once, normally — don't stretch it and don't point at the tile. If they are stuck, ask “what did you hear at the start / in the middle / at the end?” rather than naming the letter.
