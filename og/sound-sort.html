For the grown-up

Sorting beats memorising because the child generates the rule from the evidence. When they get one wrong, don't give the answer — ask “what comes just before the ending, a short vowel or something else?” The rule is printed at the top of every round on purpose: refer back to it out loud each time, in the same words, until they say it before you do.
